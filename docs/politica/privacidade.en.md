# Privacy Policy — Forge Street

Version `<<PREENCHER: 1.0>>` · effective `<<PREENCHER: date>>`

## Summary

- Forge Street has no account, no login and no chat. We don't ask for your name, email or date of birth.
- Your progress stays **on your device**. We don't run a server of our own.
- The game shows ads **that you choose to watch** in exchange for a bonus `<<CONFIRMAR: P3, revisit if ads between screens are turned on>>`. Those ads are delivered by Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads), which collects some device data, as explained below.
- To tune the game and fix bugs we use Unity services (Unity Gaming Services). **The game only sends usage statistics and error reports from players who are 18 or older and agreed.**
- Purchases are optional, go through Google Play, and the content of each one is fixed and shown before you pay.
- The first time an ad bonus shows up, the game asks for your **age range**. For players under 18, the game asks partners for non-personalized ads and doesn't use the advertising ID.
- Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on the device.

## 2. What the game stores on your device

- **Progress** (gold, upgrades, districts, goals) and a backup copy of it.
- **Settings** (music, sound effects, vibration).
- **Your age screen answer**: the **range** ("Under 13", "13 to 17" or "18 or older") and, for 18+, whether you accepted personalized ads and statistics.
- **Purchases**: the receipt and transaction number of each purchase, so the game knows what you bought and can restore it later.

This data stays on the device and we have no access to it. It lets the game pick up where you left off, apply the rules for your age and deliver your purchases.
`<<CONFIRMAR: if Android backup is enabled in the app>>` Android itself may copy these files to your Google account through the system backup service, so you can recover your progress on a new phone. This is controlled in Android's settings.

## 3. Ads and what our partners collect

The game's ads come from **Unity** (**LevelPlay** mediation and the **Unity Ads** network). The ad service only starts **after** you answer the age screen. From then on, Unity may collect:

- technical data about the device and app (for example, model, OS and game version) and diagnostics;
- approximate location, estimated from the IP address (never precise GPS location);
- device identifiers, including the Android **advertising ID**, **only for players 18 or older**;
- how you interact **with the ad** (views and taps inside the ad);
- purchase information, according to Unity Ads' own disclosure.

Purposes: showing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, keeping things secure and, **only for adults who agreed**, personalizing ads. Unity handles this data under its own policy: `<<CONFIRMAR link: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>>`.

We don't sell personal data. For players who didn't accept personalized ads (and for everyone under 18), the game tells our partners this data **may not be sold or used for personalization**.

## 4. Game configuration, statistics and errors (Unity Gaming Services)

We use **Unity Gaming Services** as a service provider (it processes data on our behalf):

- **Remote configuration** (for everyone, after the age screen): the game downloads the current settings, such as events and balance. For this, Unity receives an **installation identifier** created by the service itself, technical device and app data (model, OS, game version) and the IP address.
- **Usage statistics and error reports** (**only 18+ who agreed**): game events (for example, district opened, order delivered, purchase completed, without the transaction number), the game version, technical device data, the installation identifier and, if the game hits an error, a technical description of it. They help us understand what to improve and fix bugs. Players under 18 or who declined send none of this. `<<CONFIRMAR: D-PRIV-8, the Unity engine may send technical hardware statistics from everyone>>`

## 5. In-game purchases

Purchases are optional and go through **Google Play**. Google processes the payment: we never see your card or bank details. The game only receives the purchase receipt from Google Play, which stays on the device (section 2), to deliver the content and allow **Restore purchases**. The content of each purchase is fixed and shown before you pay. There are no mystery boxes or paid random draws. For purchases by minors, the approval controls of the Google account itself apply (Family Link).

## 6. Your age and your choices

| Your answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| Under 13 | contextual ads only (based on the game's content, not on you), no advertising ID, only networks approved for children, and the child signal (COPPA) sent to partners; no usage statistics |
| 13 to 17 | non-personalized ads with no advertising ID; no usage statistics |
| 18 or older | you choose: **Accept** (personalized ads and usage statistics) or **Decline** (generic ads, no statistics). Both buttons carry the same weight, and declining doesn't take anything away from the game |

To change your answer: **Settings > Privacy** in the game. The change fully applies the next time you open the game. On Android you can also reset or delete the advertising ID under **Settings > Google > Ads** (the name varies by manufacturer).

## 7. Children and teenagers

The game is meant for ages 13 and up, and anyone who answers under 13 gets the protection in the table above. We follow Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Law 15.211/2025), the LGPD (art. 14) and COPPA (US):

- for anyone under 18, the game asks partners for non-personalized ads, doesn't use the advertising ID and sends no usage statistics `<<CONFIRMAR: D-PRIV-2, Topics permission in the manifest>>`;
- **no paid randomness**: the game has no mystery prize boxes (loot boxes), and what each purchase or reward gives is fixed;
- no chat and no contact between players;
- we don't ask for or store anyone's name, contact details, photo or location.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's data was collected improperly, write to the contact in section 1.

## 10. Your rights and how to ask for deletion

You have the right to confirm processing, access, correct, delete, port your data and withdraw consent, and to complain to the data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

- **Delete what the game stored:** uninstall Forge Street or use **Android Settings > Apps > Forge Street > Storage > Clear data**. Note: progress can't be recovered after this, except from an Android backup. Purchases can be restored with the **Restore purchases** button.
- **Withdraw consent:** Settings > Privacy in the game.
- **Data Unity collected** (ads and Unity Gaming Services): write to the contact in section 1 and we'll forward the request, or use Unity's privacy channel `<<CONFIRMAR link>>`. Since the game doesn't ask for a name or email, you may need to provide a device identifier so the data can be found.
- **California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** declining personalized ads (or being under 18) sends partners the "do not sell or share" signal.

## 11. Security

Data that leaves the device travels encrypted (HTTPS). The game's files stay in the app's area on your device.
