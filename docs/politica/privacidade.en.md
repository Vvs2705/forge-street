# Privacy Policy — Forge Street

Version `<<PREENCHER: 1.0>>` · effective `<<PREENCHER: date>>`

## Summary

- Forge Street has no account, no login and no chat. We don't ask for your name, email or date of birth.
- Your progress stays **only on your device**. We have no server and receive none of your data.
- The game shows ads **that you choose to watch** in exchange for a bonus. Those ads are delivered by Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads), which collects some device data, as explained below.
- On first launch, the game asks your **age range**. Players under 18 never get personalized ads.
- Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device.

## 2. What the game stores on your device

- **Progress** (gold, upgrades bought, play time) and a backup copy of it.
- **Settings** (music, sound effects, vibration).
- **Your answer to the age screen**: the **range** ("Under 13", "13 to 17" or "18 or older") and, for 18+, whether you accepted personalized ads.

This data stays on the device and we have no access to it. It lets the game continue where you left off and apply the ad rules for your age.
`<<CONFIRMAR: if Android backup is enabled for the app>>` Android itself may copy these files to your Google account through the system backup service, so you can restore your progress on a new phone. You control this in Android settings.

## 3. Ads and what our partners collect

The game's ads come from **Unity** (**LevelPlay** mediation and the **Unity Ads** network). The ad service only starts **after** you answer the age screen. From then on, Unity may collect:

- technical device and app data (for example, model, operating system and game version) and performance diagnostics;
- approximate location, estimated from your IP address (never your exact GPS location);
- device identifiers, including the Android **advertising ID**, **only for players 18 or older**;
- how you interact **with the ad** (views and taps inside the ad, not inside the game).

Purposes: showing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, keeping the service secure and, **only for adults who accepted**, personalizing ads. Unity handles this data under its own policy: `<<CONFIRMAR link: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>>`.

We do not sell personal data. For players who did not accept personalized ads (and for everyone under 18), the game tells our partners that this data **may not be sold or used for personalization**.

## 4. Your age and your choices

| Your answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| Under 13 | contextual ads only (based on the game, not on you), no advertising ID, only networks approved for children, and the child signal (COPPA) sent to partners |
| 13 to 17 | non-personalized ads, no advertising ID |
| 18 or older | you choose: **Accept** (personalized ads) or **Decline** (generic ads). Both buttons look the same, and declining takes nothing away from the game |

To change your answer: **Settings > Privacy** in the game. The change fully applies the next time you open the game. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in **Settings > Google > Ads** (the name varies by manufacturer).

## 5. Children and teenagers

We follow the US COPPA, Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Law 15.211/2025), Brazil's LGPD (art. 14) and the GDPR:

- no personalized ads and no advertising ID for anyone under 18;
- **nothing random for money**: the game has no loot boxes, and what it gives is fixed;
- no chat and no contact between players;
- we don't ask for or store anyone's name, contact details, photo or location.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's data was collected improperly, write to the contact in section 1.

## 6. Legal bases (GDPR and LGPD) `<<CONFIRMAR with legal>>`

- Non-personalized ads, measurement and fraud prevention: **legitimate interests**, always with the extra protection for minors in section 5.

## 7. How long

- Data on your device: until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.
- Data Unity collects: for the period in Unity's policy.
- We keep nothing, because we receive nothing.

## 8. Your rights and how to request deletion

You have the right to know whether your data is processed, to access, correct, delete and port it, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD; in the EU, your local authority).

- **Delete what the game stored:** uninstall Forge Street or use **Android Settings > Apps > Forge Street > Storage > Clear data**. Note: progress cannot be recovered after that, except from an Android backup.
- **Withdraw personalized ads:** Settings > Privacy in the game.
- **Data Unity collected:** use Unity's privacy request channel `<<CONFIRMAR link>>` or write to us and we will forward your request. Since we receive no data, we cannot identify you on our side: the request has to reach Unity.
- **California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** declining personalized ads (or being under 18) sends partners a "do not sell or share" signal.

## 9. Security

Our ad partners encrypt data in transit. The game's files stay in the app's own storage on your device.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date and version at the top change too. If a change affects your choices, the game will ask you again.
